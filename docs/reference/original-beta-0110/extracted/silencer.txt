I. BACKGROUND
=============

Silencer is a multiplayer action strategy game that offers both solo and team
play. As a Silencer you work for one of five information hungry agencies on a
future Mars colony. Information piracy is the name of the game as you hack
your way through terminals and routers to uncover government secrets. Be
careful though because government security officers will try to stop you and
other Silencers will want to take the data pack off your corpse. Collect three
top secrets from the government and get beamed away from the colony just as
the government "cleanses" it.

III. PLAYING THE GAME
=====================

The best way to learn the game is to first play the Tutorials then jump online.
However, here's a brief synopsis for the truly eager.

If you are the team leader or playing alone you will start with a base door
builder in your inventory (upper right). Silencer bases exist in another
dimension; these special doors enable access to them. The first thing you'll
want to do is find a good place for your base door. It will remain invisible
to enemy agents until they see it used (either someone entering or exiting
the door). Once a base has been discovered by enemy agents they can enter
freely and take down systems if they have viruses or bombs. You in turn can
activate base defenses to discourage would-be saboteurs.  If you selected the
right technology before starting the game you can buy a new base door builder
and effectively rehide it.

So now you've built your base door. On the left side of the screen you'll
notice that there is a list of items.  Terminals and routers in the playfield
will come online gradually.  They show up as white blips on the radar map and
as green devices in the playfield.  As you hack terminals (big) and routers 
(small) the items in this list will turn green. When all of them are green
you will know the terminal that will be receiving a government secret. It
takes about 65 seconds for the secret to show up so you have time to prepare
to meet the opposition. After about 20 seconds your enemies will know of the
location too since all the agencies eventually know what the others are doing.
Only you team has the access code to steal the secret so only your team
members can get it from the terminal. Once it's in your possession though it
becomes free game. When you die the data pack containing the secret falls to
the ground and anyone can pick it up.

Disguising is very important in the game. Your suit has the ability to
project the image of a civilian. As a civilian government security forces will
ignore you. However, you will not be able to perform any non-civilian feats
while disguised and using a weapon or offensive inventory item will drop your
disguise. While disguised you will automatically walk like a civilian. You
can run like one by holding the Left or Right key and you can stop by holding
the Duck key.

IV. AGENCIES
============

Noxis
-----
The Noxis corporation terraformed the majority of the initial habitable
sectors of Mars, and continues to do so.  It is also responsible for producing
and selling 70 percent of the breathable oxygen. Since they are widely known
to the populace and government, they have taken steps to bolster their agent's
health so they are better able to avoid detection. Training in bio-sporria
rich environments, and possessing suits with advanced oxygen processors and
filters, has given these agents improved physical abilities such as higher
jumps, more stamina, and enhanced durability.

- Jump +5
- Endurance +3


Lazarus
-------
Like the mythical phoenix, the loose organization known as Lazarus has been
resurfacing every hundred years and causing chaos in the larger urban areas of
Mars. Whether it's belief in some higher power, an understanding of mystical
truths, or something else entirely, no one outside of the group is sure of
their motives. Numerous witnesses of Lazarus attacks claim to have seen
supposedly fatally wounded agents dust themselves off and continue their
efforts, virtually undamaged.

- Laz Tract Technology
- Resurrection (% chance of respawning at location of death)


Caliber
-------
Looking more like weekend fact-finders or trivia buffs than technological
spelunkers, members of Caliber seem to be concerned with obtaining a broad
range of general information. So while harvesting data may be only a hobby for
their predominantly upper-class agents, even the simplest router checks give
them what they want. For some reason, Caliber pays its members extremely well
for even the most common files.

- Security Badge Technology
- Contacts +3


Static
------
The Guv first realized that there was a brain-drain about four years ago.
Silently, the youngest talented computer specialists and high level
programmers were disappearing and leaving the Guv without the technical
support it required. They ended up creating Static as a technologically savvy
anti-government agency, but many members are more interested in reinforcing
their reputation as turbulently wild hackers with the best skills and
techniques.

- Virus Technology
- Periodic blips of enemy agents
- Hacking +3


Black Rose
----------
More like a dark force than a loose collection of humans, little is known of
Black Rose. Something vibrant was taken from these people, and left in its
place was a new form of sustenance. Its members seem to be misanthropic and
have a penchant for using illegal compounds such as Hollowhead injections to
cause extreme pain and internal withering in their victims. Shunned by even
the most avaristic mercenaries, these agents work alone and yet still prosper
due to some combination of ego and superlative talent.

- Poison Technology
- Poison Flare Technology
- Stealth
- Shield +2


==========
V. WEAPONS
==========

Blaster
-------
The Blaster is the main stay of all Silencers.  It derives an unlimited supply
of ammo from the Silencer's suit.  Relatively ineffective against shield
opponents the Blaster does significant damage to flesh once the shield is
gone.

Laser
-----
Immediately after shield technology was capable of dampening weapon effects,
the Frost-Light laser was released. Two hits should remove any standard
shield, but no damage is done to an unshielded target.

Rockets
-------
Long range, high yield mini-warheads will devestate an unshielded opponent.
The last best thing. A couple well timed rockets will send any Silencer back
to the Reconstructor in his base.

Flamer
------
No shield has been made that the Crucible flamer cannot ignore. It has a
rather limited but is very deadly within that range.


==============
VI. Inventory
==============

Health Pack (Noxis)
-------------------
A small portable boost for Noxis agents in the field. Restores lost health,
but must be manually applied.

Laz Tract (Lazarus)
-------------------
The definitive and unquestionable truth about Mars' oddest and most powerful
religious organization. Helpful in converting disbelieving citizens to your
cause.

Security Badge (Caliber)
------------------------
Unlimited security access. Government agents will ignore you in the field.

Virus (Static)
--------------
A portable virus whipped up by the snotty underage hackers of Static. Gives
you immediate control over cannons and robots in the field. May harm enemy
in-base weapon stations.

Poison (Black Rose)
-------------------
Hollowhead poisons are pernicious bio-rad toxins, administered via injection.
Causes the victim to lose health until cured, multiple doses recommended.

Poison Flare (Black Rose)
-------------------------
A more devious strain of the Orion Flare, the Poison Flare incapacitates its
victims in seconds and leaves them poisoned as well. Environmentally
disastrous, but highly lethal.

Plasma Bomb
-----------
An indescriminate terrorist device capable of killing even a perfectly
healthy opponent. A core explosion with multiple shrapnel tendrils, extremely
lethal.

Shaped Bomb
-----------
Like the plasma bomb but is engineered to direct its blast upwards.

EMP Bomb
--------
Sends an EMP pulse throughout the area dropping all shields.

Nuetron Bomb
------------
Kills every living thing on the level.  The only defense against it is to be
inside a base.

Detonator
---------
A remote detonation device. Deploy in strategic locations and hit FIRE to
detonate.  Comes equipped with a remote camera.

Laser Cannon
------------
A stationary laser turret with excellent offensive and defensive capabilities.
This mechanical friend in the field can do your work for you.

Flare
-----
The Orion Flare is a stationary mini-torch looks like a bomb in flight but
has a more lingering effect. Useful for blocking off tight areas or herding
enemies.

Camera
------
A remote viewing device that allows you to monitor important location on the
map.

Base Door Builder
-----------------
When secrecy is a way of life. The ability to relocate the warp door to your
base.

Base Defense
------------
Internal security systems to deter would-be ambushers. In-base laser turrets.
Multiple purchases increase turret structure durability.

Insider Info
------------
Not to be confused with "Inside Contacts" this establishes a rather expensive
link with moles inside the government.  You can purchase from them additional
information to more quickly discover the location of the next secret.

Credit Transfer
---------------
Allows you to share credits with your teammates if they find themselves a
little low on cash.


==================
VII. CHAT COMMANDS
==================

Whisper (main lobby only)
-------
	Once in whisper mode you continue to whisper to the player until you 
	issue a null whisper command (don't include the username or message)

	/w [username] [message]
	/whisper [username] [message]
	
Ignore (main lobby only)
------
	Ignores messages from the specified user

	/i [username]
	/ignore [username]

Listen (main lobby only)
------
	Resume listening to a previously ignored user

	/l [username]
	/listen [username]

Notification (main lobby only)
------------
	Toggles the "Entered / Left" system messages

	/notify

User List (main lobby only)
---------
	Displays sorted list of users in lobby
	
	/list

Emote (both lobbies)
-----
	Puts message into *[yourname] [message]* format

	/me [message]
	/emote [message]

Agent Stats (game lobby only)
-----------
	Displays an agent's stats in the chat window

	/stats [agentname]
